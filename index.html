Hire the whole café

East London born and rooted in the neighbourhood. Come for coffee , stay for lunch , get some work done at the communal table or watch Shoreditch go by from the window.

We work with brilliant independent suppliers and believe great hospitality should make everyone feel like a regular, even on their first visit.
